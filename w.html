About Floor Mats

Floor Mats for Safety, Cleaning and Lasting Performance

Floors Nigeria offers a wide variety of floor mats that cater to both commercial and domestic settings. Whether you’re looking for a door mat for your home or a large format entrance mat for your business, we have got you covered. Our range of floor mats is extensive, and you can be sure to find the perfect solution for your needs.

Dirt Trapper Mats for Homes & Businesses

Our dirt-trapping mats come in a wide range of colors and designs, making them suitable for any setting. We offer a variety of sizes, including custom-size dirt trapper mats, to cater to your specific needs. Our range of sizes includes the classic dirt trapper mat in 40×60 centimeter format to commercial entrance mats in 200×600 cm dimensions. Our dirt trapper mats are highly absorbent and have a durable backing, ensuring optimal cleaning performance and staying firmly in place while in use.

Coconut Coir Mats – The Natural Entrance Solution

Coir mats have been a popular choice for home and business entrances for many years. With their natural cleaning bristles and absorbent pile, coconut entrance mats will keep your floors fresh and clean throughout the year. Coir mats are usually placed inside floor recesses due to their thickness, which gives them a flush finish with the surrounding floor. At Floors Nigeria, we offer coir mats in a variety of sizes, thicknesses, and colors, so you can find exactly what you need.

Door Mats & Metal Entrance Mats for High Demands

Floors Nigeria offers a wide range of door mats, metal door mats, and entrance mats to cater to your needs. Our stainless steel door mats are known for their robust and reliable performance, making them ideal for both indoor and outdoor use. Metal door mats provide the best cleaning properties for even the most demanding entrances. Our metal door mats are suitable for all weather conditions, making them perfect for commercial and business settings.

If you’re looking for an ideal floor mat solution, you can be sure to find what you’re looking for in our wide range of mats at Floors Nigeria.
